Who We Are

Built for This Community

Time Now Rentals is a locally owned vehicle rental company based in Copperas Cove, Texas. We serve our local community, Fort Cavazos military families, Killeen residents, and anyone who needs a reliable, affordable vehicle right now.

We built Time Now Rentals to offer a better alternative to big-box rental agencies — real people, real vehicles, and a booking experience that is fast and transparent. No lines, no confusing fees.
