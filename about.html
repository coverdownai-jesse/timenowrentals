About Time Now Rentals

Locally owned. Community focused. Always ready.

Who We Are

Time Now Rentals is a locally owned vehicle rental company based in Copperas Cove, Texas. We serve our local community, Fort Cavazos military families, and anyone who needs a reliable, affordable vehicle right now.

We built Time Now Rentals to offer a better alternative to big-box rental agencies: real people, real vehicles, and a booking experience that is fast and transparent. No lines, no confusing fees.

Our Mission

To provide clean, reliable, and affordable vehicle rentals with insurance included, making it easy for everyone in Central Texas to get where they need to go.
